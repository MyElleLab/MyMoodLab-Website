How to Use MyMoodLab

From exporting your WhatsApp chat to getting your first analysis — in under 2 minutes.

1 Step 1

Export Your WhatsApp Chat

Two ways to get your chat into MyMoodLab. The Share Extension is faster — use it if it appears in your share sheet.

Recommended

Method A — Share Extension

  1. Open the WhatsApp chat you want to analyze
  2. Tap the contact or group name at the top
  3. Scroll down and tap "Export Chat"
  4. Choose "Without Media" (faster and smaller)
  5. In the share sheet, tap the MyMoodLab icon
  6. You'll see a confirmation — open MyMoodLab to continue

Method B — Zip File Upload

  1. Open the WhatsApp chat you want to analyze
  2. Tap the contact or group name at the top
  3. Scroll down and tap "Export Chat"
  4. Choose "Without Media"
  5. Save the .zip file to your Files app
  6. Open MyMoodLab → tap the "Add" tab
  7. Select your relationship type
  8. Tap "Upload the zipped Chat"
  9. Select the .zip file from Files
Always choose "Without Media" — MyMoodLab only analyzes text, and media files make the export much larger with no benefit to the analysis.
2 Step 2

AI Analyzes Your Conversation

Once uploaded, MyMoodLab's AI reads every message and generates a detailed relationship report.

You'll get mood scores — reciprocity, emotivity, romanticism, and green flags — plus a connection index and a daily emotional timeline.

The AI generates a "hook line" — a one-liner that captures your entire dynamic — and a "vibe verdict" labeling your relationship pattern.

Texting stats show who texts first, average response times, your longest streak, and your longest silence gap — backed by actual data from the conversation.

All analyses are saved locally on your device. Revisit them anytime from the Chats tab.

Your Data, Your Control

Here's exactly what happens with your data — and what you can do with it.

All analysis results are stored only on your device, encrypted at rest.
Chat text is anonymized — participant names are replaced with pseudonyms — before being sent for analysis.
The anonymized chat is discarded after processing. It is never stored on our servers.
Export your data: Settings → Export My Data (machine-readable JSON format)
Delete analyses: Settings → Clear All History (keeps your account intact)
Delete everything: Settings → Delete My Account (removes all local and server-side data permanently)
Manage your privacy consent at any time: Settings → Manage Privacy Consent

Tips for the Best Analysis

Small adjustments that make a real difference.

👤
Use your exact WhatsApp name

Set it in your profile so the AI correctly identifies which messages are yours in the conversation.

📁
Export without media

Text-only exports are faster and work better. Media files add size but no value to the analysis.

📅
Longer chats = better insights

A few weeks of conversation gives richer patterns than a single day. More data, more accuracy.

⚖️
Try different time windows in Court

24h for specific arguments, 7d for recent behavior, 30d for deep behavioral profiling.

💬
Ask the AI specific questions

"Is this healthy?" gets better answers than "What do you think?" Specificity is everything.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which messaging apps are supported?
Currently only WhatsApp. iMessage, Instagram, and other platforms are on the roadmap.
Is my chat stored on your servers?
No. Your chat text is sent for AI analysis and then discarded. Results are stored only on your device.
Are the other person's messages shared?
Participant names are anonymized before analysis. The AI sees "Person A" and "Person B" — not real names.
What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free users get limited monthly analyses. Pro users get higher limits and access to the Relationship Court. See our pricing in the app for details.
Can I delete my data?
Yes. Go to Settings → Clear All History to delete analyses while keeping your account, or Settings → Delete My Account to remove everything including server-side data.
How accurate is the AI analysis?
The AI provides insights based on text patterns. It's designed to be helpful and thought-provoking, not clinically accurate. Don't make major life decisions based solely on AI analysis.
Why does the app ask for my WhatsApp name?
So the AI can correctly identify which messages are yours and attribute scores, patterns, and texting stats accurately.
